Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Jibon Sathi Matrimony. Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

To help you find a life partner, we collect the following information:

  • Personal Details: Name, Age, Gender, Religion, Education, Profession, Marital Status.
  • Contact Info: Email address and Phone number.
  • Media: Profile photos and additional images you upload.
  • Location: Approximate location data (District/Lat-Long) to show nearby matches.
  • Usage Data: App activity, likes, matches, and chat history.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and display your profile to potential matches.
  • To facilitate communication (chat, voice, and video calls).
  • To personalize your experience and suggest compatible matches.
  • To ensure safety, verify profiles, and prevent fraud.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to run our app smoothly:

  • Google Firebase: For secure authentication, database storage, and push notifications.
  • Cloudinary: For secure image hosting.
  • Agora: For secure voice and video calling.
  • Google AdMob: To serve advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show personalized ads.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your chats and personal details are securely stored. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. User Rights

You have the right to edit or delete your profile at any time. If you delete your account, your personal data will be removed from our active databases.
